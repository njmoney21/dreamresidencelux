The Apartment

An Ideal Blend of
Luxury & Tradition

Nestled on the sixth floor of Zlatibor Mountain Resort & Spa, Dream Residence Lux is a fully furnished private apartment finished to the highest luxury standards. From your terrace, a breathtaking view of the lake and surrounding pine forests awaits.

Located in the very heart of Zlatibor, directly across from the iconic lake, the apartment offers the rare combination of modern comfort and authentic mountain charm. Every detail — from the Bosch kitchen to the marble bathroom — has been crafted for an exceptional stay.
